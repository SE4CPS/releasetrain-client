Reddit endpoints

Reddit section at a glance

This group covers ingestion, listing, subreddit filtering, update-related question search, CVE text search, positive-score retrieval, monthly summaries, and direct single-item fetch/update operations. Some routes use simple page/limit paging, while others also support cursor-based navigation.

Endpoints are collapsed by default and ordered by method first, then by route.

Version endpoints

Version section at a glance

There are two flavors here: older convenience routes such as /api/v/ and the newer, more controlled search route /api/v/search. For a steady client integration, /api/v/search is the safer path because its filters and paging model are explicit.

Endpoints are collapsed by default and ordered by method first, then by route.
